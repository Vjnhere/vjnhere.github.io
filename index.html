Process integration work

Two self-driven projects, built to understand how a full flow holds together rather than as coursework.

Basic 3D NAND Flash Memory Process Integration and Industry Insights

SRMIST · Nov 2025

A conceptual fabrication flow of close to 100 process steps: substrate formation, alternating oxide–nitride stacking, high-aspect-ratio channel hole etching, charge-trap layer formation, and final metallization and passivation. Each stage was simulated to see how vertical integration gets past the scaling limits of planar NAND.

Alongside the flow, I studied the industry side — the move from the first 24-layer V-NAND to 230+ layer devices, the competing architectures (TCAT, Replacement Gate, BiCS, P-BiCS, Xtacking), and the cost–yield–performance trade-offs behind them, grounded in IEDM and VLSI Symposium papers.

Monolithic 3D (M3D) Integration of Logic and Memory Devices — Process Simulation

SRMIST · May 2025

Before the integration, I worked through each unit process on its own — lithography, deposition, etching, implantation, planarization and metallization. On that base I designed and simulated a monolithic 3D IC combining a NAND-gate logic layer with a simple memory layer in SEMulator3D.

The flow covers wafer preparation, bottom-tier logic fabrication, inter-tier bonding, top-tier memory fabrication and final metallization, referencing published work on 3D IC integration and TSV-based design throughout.
